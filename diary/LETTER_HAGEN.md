# A Courier's Letter - Fort Hagen

Source for a folded letter (BOOK fc_letter_hagen). Placement candidate: on the station counter beside the cash register, inside the Fort Hagen filling station (persistent reference 001A7421, cell 0000E437). Not workshop-enabled; the earlier Red Rocket bench (near Sanctuary) and the separate office desk across the road have been discarded. Written second-to-last in reading order; first stop after Natick.

---- BTXT ----

Year: 2286

December 27th

Filling station north of the fort. Pumps still have the prices on them. Slept in the office on a pile of road maps for roads that aren't there.
Fort's up the hill. Turrets on the roof. Didn't go up.
Blood clinic across the road. Bags in the fridge, 200 years of bags. Bloodbugs outside. More inside. Sign's still bringing in customers. Left them.
Came up from Natick. Still getting used to being without it. Reach for it getting up. Nothing there. Cold goes straight through.
Left the log in Natick. House right of the roofless church. Upstairs, changing table by the crib. Don't take the street through. Mutants round the church, raiders farther on. Went round by the lake.
Told you I'd write. Never had an address. Leaving this here anyway.
North is where I'm going. Went east.
