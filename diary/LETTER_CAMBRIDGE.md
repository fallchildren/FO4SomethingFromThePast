# A Courier's Letter - Cambridge

Source for a folded letter (BOOK fc_letter_cambridge). Proposed placement: inside Cambridge Police Station, on the upright office desk with the broken terminal and fan (vanilla reference 0006ED04). Player confirmed the desk placement is suitable; normal quest-route access still needs verification.

---- BTXT ----

Year: 2287

February 17th

Cambridge. Came over the river from the ball field. Ghouls in the streets. One stood facing a wall till I stepped on glass. Went the long way after that.
Police station. Found a room with a desk, broken terminal and a fan. Newspaper on top, full ashtray, filing cabinet beside it. Slept under the desk. Could hear something breathing outside. Held my breath to listen. It stopped too.
Stayed still till morning.
Told the empty chair about the dam, the canyon, the dog. It listened better than most.
Left you a letter in Diamond City too. Room in the bar. Had a bed there. Didn't know what to do with it. Left the letter in the room.
