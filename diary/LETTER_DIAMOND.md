# A Courier's Letter - Diamond City

Source for a folded letter (BOOK fc_letter_diamond). Placement approved: on the rented bed in the Dugout Inn upstairs room (persistent vanilla reference 001395C6), not in a container. Required breadcrumb between Cambridge and Fort Hagen.

---- BTXT ----

Year: 2287

January 20th

Diamond City. A ball field with a big green wall round the back of it and a town built up inside. Came from the fort, 6 days. Left you a letter at the filling station north of the fort. Inside, on the counter by the register.
Man ahead of me at the gate was a ghoul. Guard said not you. Man said he'd lived in Boston since before the guard's grandfather. Guard said not you. Man walked back down the road. Guard looked at me, asked what I was carrying. Told him everything but the letter. I went in.
Noodles at a stand. 4 caps. Best thing I've eaten in 5 years. Ate it standing up.
Asked at 3 stalls where Sanctuary Hills is. Nobody knows it by name. One said up past the farms, off the Concord road, and didn't say how far.
Said I'd buy supplies. Bought nothing.
2 nights in a room in the bar. Slept both.
