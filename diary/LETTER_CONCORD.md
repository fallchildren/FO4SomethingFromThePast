# A Courier's Letter - Concord

Source for a folded letter (BOOK fc_letter_concord). Proposed placement: the upstairs room in the Museum of Freedom where the player first meets Preston and the settlers. Placement approved: desk beside Sturges' terminal and coffee pot (0003F403), revised inset offset (-30,8,58). The courier's stay precedes their arrival and the raider siege; the nighttime encounter hints at the vanilla deathclaw, not an additional spawned creature.

---- BTXT ----

Year: 2287

April 4th

Concord. A main street, a bridge, a museum with a flag still on the roof. Found an empty room upstairs. Enough chairs for a meeting. Had it to myself.
Came from the greenhouse, 2 days on the old road. Been here 18 since. Graygarden. All robots. Left you a letter on the red workshop bench there.
The road to Sanctuary Hills is the one off the bridge. Walked it 2 miles. Turned round. Walked 2 miles back.
Woke after midnight to metal scraping under the street. Looked down from an upstairs window. A claw came through a gap by the broken road, felt along the edge, then withdrew. Too big for anything I wanted to meet.
Stayed in the museum till morning. Quiet building. Thick walls. Appreciated both.
Tomorrow, I said. Said it at the fort too.
