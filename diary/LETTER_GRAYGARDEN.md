# A Courier's Letter - Graygarden

---- BTXT ----

Year: 2287

March 14th

A greenhouse, crops outside too, all of it tended by Mister Handys. Not a person in it. Came from the schools, 4 days. Left you a letter in Cambridge. Inside the police station, on the desk by the broken terminal. Moved a newspaper to make room. Ashtray was full. Whoever sat there kept putting off leaving too.
One floated over and asked if I'd come to help. Said no. It said that was fine, the work was getting done.
3 of them in charge, each with a color so you can tell them apart. Told me I could have the shed. Stayed 3 weeks. Pulled weeds. Held a hose. Nobody asked who I was. Nobody asked where I was going. Everyone asks where. First place in 5 years that didn't.
They gave me a tato. Hadn't had one since the Mojave.
Told one I had a letter for Sanctuary Hills. It said it knew the road. A Mr. Handy lives up there, very proud of the lawns.
Leaving in the morning. Knew all week.
