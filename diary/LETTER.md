# A Courier's Letter

Source for the first letter in the intended reading order (BOOK fc_letter_sanctuary; replaces legacy NOTE fc_tape_letter), left on the built-in kitchen counter in the Sole Survivor's house in Sanctuary. The sealed job letter is a separate delivery, left in a mailbox in the story; it is not the readable quest letter or an additional placed item. The remaining payment is conditional on someone opening the door, not a cache waiting at the address. The courier's illness and fate are implied, not diagnosed or confirmed.

---- BTXT ----

Year: 2287

April 13th

Sanctuary Hills. 209 years of nobody. A Mr. Handy at the end of the road asked if I'd seen the family. Told it no. It thanked me for my time.
Found the house number. Checked it twice.
No door.
Half now, half from whoever opens the door. That's what the man said.
Carried the job letter round this street for 3 days. Kept it dry through everything. Put it in the box tonight, wax and all. Didn't open it. Wanted to.
Came the long way. Farm back down the road feeds anybody who stops. Built under a power pylon. Left you a letter there too, on the red workshop bench.
The cough's worse indoors. Worse outside too. Was blaming the dust. Not much dust in what came up this morning.
The Mr. Handy offered water. Brought a glass for somebody who doesn't live here anymore. Held it while I drank.
Told you I'd write. Never had an address. This is it.
Walked here from the Mojave. Dead man's boots. A shovel I didn't start with. Left it where I last needed it.
Left this in the kitchen where it won't get wet.
Five years last October.
Going to rest a minute.
