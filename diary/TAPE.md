# A Courier's Log

Source for the big holotape (NOTE fc_tape_diary), left on the changing table in the house right of the church in Natick. The coat and helmet are on top of the dryer, with the shovel beside the machines. Last find in the intended reading order; its entries are the earliest part of the courier's journey.
One 'Year:' line sets the year for the dated entries that follow it; each entry is a
date heading and then paragraphs. Each nonblank prose line is a paragraph; terminal
pages separate paragraphs with two newlines. Long entries use numbered continuation
pages, split at paragraph boundaries. The prebuilt ESP must be updated separately
when this source changes; the release packaging step does not render diary text.

Year: 2281

October 31st

Got the coat from you. Seen Veteran Rangers wearing its like. Different colors. Green glass instead of red. Camouflage on the plates under the coat.
Not just a coat. Calling it a coat.
Got the job from a man who wouldn't give his name. A letter, sealed, wax on it. Address in the Commonwealth. Street and house number. No name. Half now, half from whoever opens the door. More than I'd see in ten years. Everyone I asked said where?
Left the same afternoon.
You said a man named Clark lived in that canyon 47 years. Maybe wore it the whole time. You didn't say. Said it sat in his cave another 157 after he died and nobody touched it.
You said, "It'll keep you alive."
Told you I'd write.

November 19th

The dam. Flag on it, didn't look which. Guard asked what was in the pack. Mail. He looked at the wax and let me by.
Crossed the river on foot. Water both sides. Road ran east. Stayed on it.

Year: 2282

January 6th

Went 5 days out of the way to see the canyon. Cave's where you said, up a ledge. A bed. A terminal, still lit. Didn't touch it. Dust on everything but one bare nail in the wall.
Stood there in it. Fits better than I'd like.
Went back to the road.

February 4th

Man in a ditch, belly wound, 2 days in by the look of it. Sat with him. Gave him the rest of my water. Asked about his boots while he could still answer. He said sure.
Asked where he was headed. He said home. Said it like I'd know the place. Pointed east.
Told him I'd bury him. Died at dusk. No shovel. Laid him out with his feet pointing east. Best I had.
Boots are 2 sizes small.

March 21st

6 weeks in the dead man's boots. Blood in both socks every night. None today.
Feet won.
Still east. Road keeps going.
Counted to get to sleep. Got to 141. Not saying what.

September 8th

A dog. Yellow, one ear. 40 yards back since the ford. Won't come closer. Won't go away. Not mine.
Left the bones out for it. It ate them. Didn't ask.

Year: 2283

November 3rd

Settlement of 31 took me in for the winter. Rent is digging. Dog sleeps outside the gate. 25 yards.

November 9th

Bad at digging. Told so by 3 people. One of them was a kid, 9 or 10. Calling the kid Foreman.
Foreman says I hold the shovel like it owes me money.

December 5th

Foreman's mother has a cough that isn't the cold kind. Settlement has a doctor, a man with a book.
Trader came through with antibiotics. Wanted something worth it. Settlement has nothing worth it.
I had the coat on my back and a sealed letter in my pack. He looked at the pack. Didn't ask. Didn't offer.

December 12th

Foreman's mother died at dawn. Ground is frozen 14 inches down.

December 13th

Dug 6 inches. Foreman took the shovel, said light a fire on the spot first, dig after it burns down. 9 years old and knew that.

December 14th

Buried her. 4 feet. Foreman stood at the end of it for an hour. Then said I was getting better at digging.
I wasn't.
Counted to get to sleep. Got to 774. Not saying what.

Year: 2284

March 22nd

Left the settlement. Dog at 10 yards. Foreman called it and it went.
Dog is somebody's now. Dog agreed.
Foreman gave me a shovel. Said I'd need it. Said it like a threat.

June 9th

Smoke from a hundred chimneys, all leaning the same way. A man at the bridge said they make steel there and keep the people who make it. Didn't go in. 11 days round.

Year: 2285

August 30th

Caravan, 6 brahmin, 9 people. Gave me 3 days of food. Didn't ask for anything.

Year: 2286

June 17th

Big white buildings, all of them dead. A river with a ship in it and a town in the ship. Woman at the rail asked what I was carrying. Mail. She laughed. Not unkind.

September 22nd

Sign says Commonwealth. Sat under it a while. Thought about the man in the ditch. Wondered if anybody was still waiting at home.
Motel up the road has smoke in every window. Went round. Slept in a culvert. 4 hours.

November 21st

Natick. Road in is one long wreck. Truck on its side, somebody's arm out from under the door. Went around by the lake. Half the town is under it.
Super mutants round the church. Raiders at the other end of town. Something's clawed grooves in a car door. Didn't stay in the street.
Church with no roof. House on the right of it, second floor come down through the first. Crib upstairs, mobile still hanging. Looks like nothing. Nobody loots a house that looks like that.
3 inside. 2 in a bed. One an infant. Nobody ever came for them.

November 23rd

Buried the infant behind the house. Super mutants in the church yard. Lit a fire on the spot first, dug after it burned down. Foreman's way. Ground is half rock. Took 2 days.
Left the parents in the bed. Arms round each other. Tried to lift one. Floor shifted under me. Left them together.
Had a shovel this time.
Good at it now. Nobody asked.

November 25th

Went through the other houses at first light. Checking doors, ways out. Found a terminal still running in one of them. Back to the house right of the church before dark.
Logs on the terminal. Not mine. Reading them anyway.
Kim Wu. Name's on the profile, not in the writing. Young. Can't tell boy or girl from the name. Doesn't matter.
October 2077. Excited about Halloween costumes, then a bus comes and takes the neighbours away.
Mama takes them and their brother up onto the roof to play and keeps them out there until dark. The child thinks it's a treat. Writes, "Grownups are weird."

November 26th

Next log. The child moved to Uncle Marshall's house. Hates it - no rooms, everyone in the back, smells.
Auntie Song "is going away to a camp for a while." The child thinks that sounds fun. Papa won't answer questions and tells them not to bring it up again.
That's how a child writes down an internment.

November 27th

Papa confiscates a holotape they were playing at a neighbour's - a kid's game about the Red Menace - and the child cannot work out what they did wrong.
Furious about missing the World Series.
Worst year of that family's life. Reads like a list of complaints.

December 4th

Took the helmet off to type. Set it down. Front faces the screen.
Front, in marker, over where the eyes go: FORGIVE ME MAMA.
Back: NANJING, JUN JUL AUG. SE, crossed out. SHANGHAI, SEP OCT NOV DEC JAN FEB MAR APL MAY.
Collar: S/SGT R.B. VICKERS U.S.M.C. B POS.
Maybe the same year as the roof. Different country. He's in Nanjing in June with a rifle, the child's on a roof in October hiding from people with rifles, and neither knows the other exists.
Seen all of it before. First time I've read it with somebody else's log open.

December 9th

Snow since morning. Mutants by twice. Raiders once. A deathclaw once, up the street at dusk. Stood there 20 minutes and left. None of them came in. House looks like nothing.
Job letter's in the pack. Sanctuary Hills is 3 days north. Haven't gone.
Read the logs again. Still the roof.

December 18th

Not staying. Know how it looks. Not doing what the man in the canyon did. His was his.
Slept last night without it. Woke up.
Dryer's the one door in the house still shut. Coat's folded on top. Helmet beside it, front out. Shovel stays beside the dryer.
Couldn't give the trader either one. Coat stays. Job letter goes with me. Know it's late.
Rest of the money is at the end of the road. Can't picture anybody opening that door.
Vickers, 12 months written on his helmet in his own hand. Clark, 47 years, maybe in it, then the cave. You, never asked. Me, 5 years and a month.
You asked if I was going to be alright. I said yes. That was the first thing I got wrong.
Counted to get to sleep every night since. 1,874 tonight. Days. That's what it was.
Going to make it.
