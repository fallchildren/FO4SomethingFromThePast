# A Courier's Letter - Abernathy Farm

Source for a folded letter (BOOK fc_letter_abernathy). Proposed placement: on top of the red settlement workshop bench (persistent vanilla reference 0006F5C5). Not inside its shared inventory. Workshop bench placement approved in game. Second note the player reads.

---- BTXT ----

Year: 2287

April 9th

Farm on the road before Sanctuary Hills. Tatos and melons. A couple, 2 grown daughters, a dog that came right up and licked my hand. Wrong dog.
Farmer fed me. Asked where I was headed. Told him. He said nobody's up there. There's a Mr. Handy who keeps the lawns and talks to the houses. Said it's no trouble but don't go at night, it's got a flamethrower.
Younger daughter asked who the letter was from. Don't know. Who it's to. Don't know that either. She said then why. Got paid, I said. She said that's not a reason.
Farmer offered to carry it up himself. 20 minutes. Said no. Don't know why. Sat on the porch till dark with it in my hand.
Left you a letter in Concord. Museum with the flag on the roof, in the room upstairs with all the chairs. Desk by the terminal and coffee pot. Stayed there longer than I meant to.
Going up tomorrow.
